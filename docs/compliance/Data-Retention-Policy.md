# Data Retention Policy

## Purpose

Define data-retention readiness controls for customer, credit, legal, financial, audit, upload, and operational records.

## Scope

Applies to Credit-Eoscar records, BrandonFintech integration planning, uploaded documents, audit logs, customer agreements, payment records, credit data, and admin workflows.

## Applicable Module

Document Room, customer onboarding, credit workflows, payments, audit logs, uploads, admin records, and infrastructure backups.

## Required Controls

- Define retention periods by record type.
- Separate active records, archived records, deleted records, and legal holds.
- Preserve audit logs for high-risk actions.
- Mask or delete sensitive data when retention expires and deletion is permitted.
- Do not delete records subject to legal hold, dispute, audit, or contract obligation.

## Manual Tasks

- Approve retention schedule with counsel.
- Assign data owners.
- Define archive and deletion procedures.
- Periodically review records past retention.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Backup storage.
- Secure deletion and archive tooling.

## Evidence Needed

- Retention schedule.
- Deletion logs.
- Archive logs.
- Legal hold records.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires legal/compliance approval before production enforcement.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not determine lawful retention or deletion duties.
