# FCRA Compliance

## Purpose

Document FCRA readiness controls for credit report access, consumer authorization, dispute workflows, data accuracy, permissible purpose, and evidence retention.

## Scope

Applies to Credit-Eoscar credit report workflows, dispute workflows, credit analysis, onboarding, bureau readiness, and customer authorization records.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar credit module, credit report authorization, dispute authorization, Metro 2 readiness, e-OSCAR readiness, Document Room, and audit logs.

## Required Controls

- Obtain and retain required consumer authorization before credit report access.
- Track permissible-purpose basis and reviewer.
- Protect credit report data with strict access controls.
- Maintain dispute records, consumer instructions, evidence, and submission history.
- Do not claim bureau production access or FCRA compliance without verified authorization and legal review.

## Manual Tasks

- Approve credit report authorization language.
- Configure bureau provider credentials only through secrets.
- Define retention and deletion rules.
- Review dispute and report access workflows with counsel.

## External Dependencies

- Bureau credentials and authorization.
- Legal/compliance review.
- Secure storage and audit logging.

## Evidence Needed

- Signed authorizations.
- Permissible-purpose records.
- Bureau configuration evidence.
- Access logs.
- Dispute history.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires legal review and authorized bureau access before production credit-report use.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not authorize access to consumer reports.
