# BSA Compliance Readiness

## Purpose

Define Bank Secrecy Act readiness considerations for future partner-bank, payment, lending, or account-like workflows.

## Scope

Applies only to workflows that may require BSA controls after legal, partner, and compliance review.

## Applicable Module

BrandonFintech future account/payment modules, Credit-Eoscar compliance workflows, audit logs, admin escalation, and partner-readiness documentation.

## Required Controls

- Identify whether the product or partner relationship triggers BSA obligations.
- Map required controls to KYC, KYB, CIP, sanctions, transaction monitoring, audit logging, and escalation.
- Maintain evidence of manual review and escalation.
- Avoid representing BSA compliance as complete until approved by qualified professionals and partners.

## Manual Tasks

- Complete legal applicability analysis.
- Approve compliance program scope.
- Assign compliance owners.
- Define monitoring, reporting, and record retention procedures.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Banking/payment partner requirements.
- Monitoring tools, if applicable.

## Evidence Needed

- Applicability memo.
- Policy approvals.
- Training records.
- Monitoring and escalation records.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires external compliance review and partner alignment.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not establish BSA compliance or regulatory approval.
