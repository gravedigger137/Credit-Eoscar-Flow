# Dispute Authorization

## Purpose

Document controls for customer authorization to prepare, manage, or submit credit dispute workflows.

## Scope

Applies to Credit-Eoscar dispute workflows, bureau communications, e-OSCAR readiness, customer evidence, support communications, and admin review.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar disputes, e-OSCAR readiness, Document Room, customer onboarding, support inbox, compliance review, and audit logs.

## Required Controls

- Capture customer authorization before dispute workflow activity.
- Track scope, authorization version, evidence, disputed item, customer instructions, and status.
- Preserve audit logs for each dispute action and reviewer.
- Require manual review before any production bureau or e-OSCAR submission.
- Do not claim e-OSCAR or bureau submission unless actually transmitted through authorized channels.

## Manual Tasks

- Review dispute authorization template with counsel.
- Define evidence requirements.
- Configure bureau/e-OSCAR credentials only after authorization.
- Train admins on submission boundaries.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- Bureau/e-OSCAR credentials and contracts, if used.
- Secure storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Signed authorization.
- Customer evidence.
- Dispute item records.
- Submission logs, if applicable.
- Audit trail.

Draft. Requires legal review and authorized transmission channels before live submission.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not authorize bureau or e-OSCAR submissions.
