# Metro 2 Readiness

## Purpose

Document readiness controls for Metro 2 workflow support, formatting review, data accuracy, authorization, and production-transmission boundaries.

## Scope

Applies to Credit-Eoscar Metro 2 workflow support, credit-data normalization, furnisher-readiness planning, internal analytics, and audit records.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar credit workflows, Metro 2 readiness, bureau integration planning, compliance review, data quality, and audit logs.

## Required Controls

- Treat Metro 2 output as readiness or test workflow until production authorization is documented.
- Validate data accuracy, source evidence, account ownership, reporting obligations, and furnisher requirements before any live use.
- Maintain versioned format rules and audit logs.
- Require human review before export or transmission.
- Do not represent Metro 2 production reporting as active without approved credentials and agreements.

## Manual Tasks

- Confirm furnisher status and obligations.
- Approve data mapping with compliance professionals.
- Validate test files before external submission.
- Document bureau-specific requirements.

## External Dependencies

- Bureau/furnisher agreements.
- Compliance review.
- Secure transmission channel, if approved.

## Evidence Needed
