# Compliance Gap Register

## Purpose

Track compliance, security, infrastructure, legal, vendor, bureau, payment, and operational gaps that must be resolved before staging, production, or regulated use.

## Scope

Applies to all compliance documents in this folder, Credit-Eoscar workflows, BrandonFintech integration planning, infrastructure readiness, payment readiness, bureau readiness, and admin operations.

## Applicable Module

Compliance operations, admin review, Document Room, infrastructure, payments, credit workflows, onboarding, and audit logs.

| Gap ID | Area | Gap | Severity | Owner | Status | Evidence Needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| GAP-002 | Bureau access | Production bureau/e-OSCAR access not claimed | High | Credit ops owner | Open | Approved credentials |

## Manual Tasks

- Add every unresolved blocker to this register.
- Assign owners and due dates.
- Link evidence when closed.
- Review before go-live decisions.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Vendor evidence.
- Production credentials.
- Infrastructure configuration.

## Evidence Needed

- Closure notes.
- Approval records.
- Test evidence.
- External authorization evidence.
- Audit logs.
