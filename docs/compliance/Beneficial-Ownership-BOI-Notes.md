# Beneficial Ownership / BOI Notes

## Purpose

Document the readiness controls for collecting and maintaining beneficial ownership information and BOI-related records without exposing sensitive identifiers in the application or repository.

## Scope

Applies to entity onboarding, business registration records, trust-owned business records, corporate records, customer KYB, vendor onboarding, and due-diligence packages.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar onboarding, Document Room, Asset Register, corporate records, trust records, BrandonFintech future KYB workflows, and admin compliance review.

## Required Controls

- Mask EINs, tax IDs, dates of birth, addresses, and government identifiers where not required in the UI.
- Store source evidence in private storage with role-based access.
- Track document owner, review status, version, and access history.
- Require manual review before BOI records are included in lender, investor, vendor, or bank partner packages.
- Avoid claiming BOI filing completion unless filing evidence exists.

## Manual Tasks

- Confirm current BOI requirements with qualified counsel or compliance advisor.
- Upload filing receipts only to protected storage.
- Maintain current beneficial-owner roster and supporting documents.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Government filing system availability.
- Private storage and access logging.

## Evidence Needed

- Entity formation records.
- Ownership records.
- Filing receipts where applicable.
- Audit logs showing reviewer and date.

Draft. Requires legal/compliance review and source evidence before operational use.
