# Social Media Asset Register

## Purpose
Catalog social accounts and brand assets.

## Scope
Handles, URLs, content ownership, access custodian, MFA status, and last audit date.

## Owner
Social Media Review Agent.

## Human Review Required
Financial, lending, legal, credit, affiliate, or testimonial claims.

## Security Notes
Use password managers and MFA. Do not commit login details.

## Audit Requirements
Record access changes, ownership changes, and campaign launches.

## Related Routes
None currently.

## Related Database Tables
`document_room_items`, `audit_events`.

## Go/No-Go Criteria
All active accounts must have MFA and approved brand guidelines.
