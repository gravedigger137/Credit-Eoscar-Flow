# AML Readiness

## Purpose

Document anti-money-laundering readiness controls for future regulated workflows while avoiding claims that the platform is a bank, MSB, lender, broker, or approved compliance program.

## Scope

Applies to payments, customer onboarding, trust-accounting metadata, future account workflows, receivable review, vendor review, and suspicious-activity escalation readiness.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar compliance workflows, BrandonFintech payments and ledger planning, admin review, audit logs, and future partner-bank or payment-provider integrations.

## Required Controls

- Identify workflows that may trigger AML obligations before launch.
- Track risk indicators, source of funds evidence where applicable, sanctions-screening status, and review outcomes.
- Maintain audit logs for manual review and escalation decisions.
- Separate internal readiness scoring from regulated determinations.
- Do not claim AML program completion without counsel, compliance approval, and partner requirements.

## Manual Tasks

- Determine whether regulated AML obligations apply.
- Define risk scoring and escalation criteria.
- Approve suspicious-activity handling procedures with counsel.
- Train authorized staff before production use.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Sanctions screening provider.
- Banking/payment partner requirements.
- Monitoring and audit retention.

## Evidence Needed

- AML policy approval.
- Risk assessment.
- Escalation records.
- Provider configuration evidence.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires legal/compliance review and partner-specific requirements before production use.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not establish a compliant AML program by itself.
