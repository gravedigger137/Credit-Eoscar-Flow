# Campaign Operations

## Purpose
Define campaign planning, review, launch, monitoring, and archive workflow.

## Scope
Social, email, SMS, blog, SEO, affiliate, and referral campaigns.

## Owner
Marketing Operations Agent.

## Human Review Required
Compliance disclosures, regulated claims, partner claims, affiliate restrictions, and customer testimonials.

## Security Notes
Do not upload customer PII into marketing tools without consent and policy review.

## Audit Requirements
Record campaign owner, approval, launch date, channels, restrictions, and results.

## Related Routes
None currently.

## Related Database Tables
`document_room_items`, `audit_events`.

## Go/No-Go Criteria
Campaigns require approved copy, disclosure review, and platform access review.
