# CIP Readiness

## Purpose

Document Customer Identification Program readiness for future regulated account or payment workflows.

## Scope

Applies to BrandonFintech future account-like workflows, payment-related onboarding, Credit-Eoscar client onboarding where identity evidence is required, and partner-bank readiness.

## Applicable Module

Shared identity planning, Credit-Eoscar onboarding, BrandonFintech future banking-style modules, compliance review, and audit logs.

## Required Controls

- Define what identifying information must be collected for each workflow.
- Verify identity using approved methods before claiming completion.
- Maintain records of verification method, date, reviewer, and outcome.
- Escalate incomplete, inconsistent, or high-risk records.
- Keep CIP readiness separate from production regulated account approval.

## Manual Tasks

- Determine whether CIP obligations apply to each product.
- Approve required data fields and evidence sources.
- Configure provider integrations or manual verification procedures.
- Review records before production activation.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Identity verification provider or approved manual procedure.
- Bank or payment partner requirements.

## Evidence Needed

- Identity evidence.
- Verification records.
- Policy approval.
- Reviewer notes.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires compliance approval and provider or manual process setup.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not create a complete CIP program by itself.
