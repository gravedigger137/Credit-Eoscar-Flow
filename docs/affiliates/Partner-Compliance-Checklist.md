# Partner Compliance Checklist

## Purpose
Review partner and affiliate readiness before marketing or integration.

## Scope
Affiliate programs, vendor partners, marketplace partners, and referral programs.

## Owner
Vendor Management Agent.

## Human Review Required
Contract terms, data-sharing, payment terms, taxes, regulated marketing, and brand usage.

## Security Notes
Do not expose affiliate IDs, payout account numbers, tax IDs, or API keys in public docs.

## Audit Requirements
Record approval status and compliance restrictions.

## Related Routes
None currently.

## Related Database Tables
`document_room_items`, `audit_events`.

## Go/No-Go Criteria
Partner must be approved, documented, and reviewed before use.
