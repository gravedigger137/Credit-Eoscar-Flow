# E-Signature Readiness

## Purpose

Document readiness controls for electronic signature capture, identity association, document integrity, versioning, and execution status.

## Scope

Applies to customer agreements, authorization forms, corporate documents, trust records, promissory note drafts, security agreement drafts, and onboarding documents.

## Applicable Module

Document Room, customer onboarding, legal workflow support, compliance review, audit logs, and future e-signature provider integration.

## Required Controls

- Keep document generation separate from execution status.
- Track signer identity, document version, timestamp, completion status, and audit evidence.
- Store signed documents in protected storage.
- Require attorney review for documents intended for legal effect.
- Do not claim execution or enforceability merely because a document was generated.

## Manual Tasks

- Select e-signature provider or approved manual signing workflow.
- Review signature language and evidence requirements with counsel.
- Define signer authentication requirements.
- Test document integrity and audit trails.

## External Dependencies

- E-signature provider, if used.
- Legal review.
- Secure storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Signed document.
- Signature audit certificate or equivalent.
- Template version.
- Signer authentication record.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires e-signature workflow and legal review before production reliance.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not determine whether an electronic signature is valid or enforceable.
