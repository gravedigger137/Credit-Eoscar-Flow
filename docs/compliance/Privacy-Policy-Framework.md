# Privacy Policy Framework

## Purpose

Document privacy-policy readiness requirements for customer disclosures, data categories, processing purposes, sharing, retention, rights requests, and security controls.

## Scope

Applies to public site, customer onboarding, Credit-Eoscar workflows, BrandonFintech integration planning, AI workflows, payments, uploads, and vendor integrations.

## Applicable Module

Public pages, onboarding, Document Room, customer records, AI workflows, payments, vendor management, and compliance review.

## Required Controls

- Maintain current privacy policy version and acceptance records.
- Disclose data categories and processing purposes accurately.
- Track vendors and subprocessors.
- Define rights-request intake and response workflow.
- Avoid claiming privacy certifications or compliance statuses not obtained.

## Manual Tasks

- Review privacy policy with counsel.
- Map data categories and vendors.
- Approve retention and sharing disclosures.
- Train admins on privacy requests.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- Vendor security documentation.
- Rights-request workflow tooling.

## Evidence Needed

- Approved privacy policy.
- Version history.
- Customer acceptance records.
- Vendor inventory.
- Rights-request logs.

Draft. Requires legal review before publication or production reliance.
