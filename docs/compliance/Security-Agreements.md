# Security Agreements

## Purpose

Define controls for security-agreement records and collateral-reference workflows.

## Scope

Covers secured party, debtor, collateral description, software/IP/domain/database/contracts/receivables categories, governing law, supporting documents, review status, execution tracking, and audit history.

## Applicable Module

Legal Instruments, Collateral Assets, Master Asset Register, Document Room, Credit Facility Readiness.

## Required Controls

- Store collateral categories as records, not legal conclusions.
- Keep collateral values separate from descriptions and require review.
- Require governing law and secured-party fields.
- Preserve version history for security-agreement drafts.
- Require attorney review before approval, lender visibility, or external use.
- Audit collateral-value changes, lender-visible flags, and approval status.

## Manual Tasks

- Confirm debtor authority and asset ownership evidence.
- Review collateral sufficiency and perfection requirements with counsel.
- Link supporting documents.
- Confirm whether UCC filing is required.

## External Dependencies

- Legal counsel.
- UCC filing system or filing service.
- Asset documentation and storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Draft security agreement.
- Asset register links.
- Ownership/supporting documents.
- Review approvals.
- Filing evidence if filed.
- Audit trail.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires external legal review.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not create, perfect, or enforce a security interest.
