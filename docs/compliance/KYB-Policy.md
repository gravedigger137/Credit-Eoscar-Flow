# KYB Policy

## Purpose

Define Know Your Business readiness controls for business entity verification, ownership review, authority review, and risk escalation.

## Scope

Applies to business customers, vendors, trust-owned businesses, corporate records, partner onboarding, and future BrandonFintech business-account workflows.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar onboarding, corporate records, trust records, Asset Register, Document Room, BrandonFintech future business onboarding, and compliance review queues.

## Required Controls

- Collect formation records, business registration records, authority records, ownership records, and tax documentation only through protected workflows.
- Mask sensitive identifiers.
- Track authorized signer, business status, DBA/trade names, and source evidence.
- Require manual review for ownership changes, high-risk activities, conflicting records, or missing authority evidence.
- Do not claim KYB completion without verified evidence and reviewer approval.

## Manual Tasks

- Confirm accepted business evidence by jurisdiction.
- Review ownership and control records.
- Confirm authority of signer or officer.
- Re-review entities on material changes.

## External Dependencies

- Secretary of State or registry sources.
- KYB provider, if used.
- Legal/compliance review.

## Evidence Needed

- Formation documents.
- Good standing or registration evidence.
- Ownership records.
- Signer authority evidence.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires compliance approval and evidence-source decisions.
