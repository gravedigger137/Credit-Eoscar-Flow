# ECOA Compliance

## Purpose

Document Equal Credit Opportunity Act readiness boundaries for workflows that may support future lending, underwriting support, or receivable review.

## Scope

Applies to internal readiness scoring, receivable review, future lending workflows, customer risk review, and any process that could influence credit-related decisions.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar internal analytics, BrandonFintech future lending planning, underwriting-support documentation, audit logs, and compliance review.

## Required Controls

- Keep internal readiness scores separate from loan approval or adverse-action decisions.
- Require professional review before any workflow is used in regulated lending.
- Maintain reasons, data inputs, reviewer notes, and audit logs for high-risk decisions.
- Avoid prohibited-basis data use unless counsel approves a compliant purpose.
- Do not generate adverse-action notices automatically.

## Manual Tasks

- Complete fair-lending review before regulated use.
- Define model governance and bias-review procedures.
- Approve data fields and decision boundaries.
- Document lender or partner requirements.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Fair-lending review.
- Lending partner requirements, if applicable.

## Evidence Needed

- Data field inventory.
- Decision-boundary approval.
- Model review records.
- Human review notes.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires legal/compliance review before any regulated lending use.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not authorize lending, underwriting, or adverse-action workflows.
