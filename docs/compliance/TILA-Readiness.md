# TILA Readiness

## Purpose

Document Truth in Lending Act readiness considerations for future credit, financing, payment-plan, or lending-related workflows.

## Scope

Applies only to future products or workflows that may disclose credit terms, finance charges, payment schedules, or consumer-credit obligations.

## Applicable Module

BrandonFintech future lending planning, Credit-Eoscar customer agreement review, payments, billing workflow, document templates, and compliance queues.

## Required Controls

- Do not present credit terms, APR, finance charges, or payment obligations without reviewed templates.
- Track governing product, disclosure version, customer acceptance, and evidence.
- Require counsel review before any consumer-credit disclosure is used.
- Keep payment-intent testing and subscription billing separate from lending disclosures.

## Manual Tasks

- Determine whether TILA applies to each product.
- Approve disclosure templates.
- Validate calculations with qualified professionals.
- Retain customer acceptance evidence.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Lending partner requirements, if applicable.
- Calculation validation procedure.

## Evidence Needed

- Approved disclosures.
- Calculation validation records.
- Customer acceptance records.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires external legal review before any TILA-covered use.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not establish compliant TILA disclosures.
