# EFTA Readiness

## Purpose

Document Electronic Fund Transfer Act readiness controls for future electronic payment, wallet, ACH, debit, or account-transfer workflows.

## Scope

Applies to future BrandonFintech electronic-transfer workflows, payment authorization, dispute handling, customer notices, and error-resolution readiness.

## Applicable Module

BrandonFintech payments and transfers planning, Credit-Eoscar billing workflow, customer authorization records, audit logs, and compliance review.

## Required Controls

- Collect and retain clear customer authorization for electronic transfers.
- Track authorization version, timing, revocation, and payment method reference.
- Provide reviewed error-resolution and dispute procedures before production use.
- Do not imply regulated electronic fund transfer compliance without partner and legal review.

## Manual Tasks

- Determine EFTA applicability.
- Approve authorization language.
- Define dispute and error-resolution processes.
- Configure payment-provider evidence retention.

## External Dependencies

- Legal/compliance review.
- Payment provider or bank partner requirements.
- E-signature or consent system, if used.

## Evidence Needed

- Transfer authorizations.
- Revocation records.
- Payment provider logs.
- Error-resolution records.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires payment-provider and legal review before covered production use.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not certify EFTA compliance.
