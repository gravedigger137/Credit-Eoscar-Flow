# UCC-1 / UCC-3 Filing Support

## Purpose

Define readiness controls for tracking UCC-1 financing statements, UCC-3 amendments/continuations/terminations, and related filing evidence.

## Scope

Covers filing workflow metadata only. The platform must not claim a filing is valid, effective, perfected, timely, or accepted unless official evidence is attached and reviewed.

## Applicable Module

Document Room, Legal Instruments, Collateral Assets, Credit Facility Readiness, Compliance.

## Required Controls

- Track filing type, jurisdiction, debtor, secured party, collateral reference, filing office, filing date, file number, and status.
- Store official acknowledgement evidence when available.
- Require attorney review before marking filing-ready or filed.
- Audit all filing status changes.
- Keep UCC-1/UCC-3 templates and filing instructions modular.

## Manual Tasks

- Confirm filing jurisdiction and debtor name rules with counsel.
- Submit filings through approved channels.
- Upload filing acknowledgements.
- Calendar continuation and lapse dates.

## External Dependencies

- State filing office.
- Filing service provider if used.
- Legal review.
- Secure document storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Filing draft.
- Filing submission receipt.
- Official acknowledgement.
- File number.
- Continuation/lapse calendar record.
- Audit log.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Requires External Approval. Filing validity depends on external filing office acceptance and legal review.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not determine perfection, priority, validity, or filing sufficiency.
