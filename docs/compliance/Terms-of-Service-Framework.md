# Terms of Service Framework

## Purpose

Document terms-of-service readiness controls for product boundaries, customer obligations, service limitations, payments, disclaimers, and version tracking.

## Scope

Applies to public signup, customer onboarding, Credit-Eoscar services, BrandonFintech future platform terms, AI workflow support, payments, and admin workflows.

## Applicable Module

Public pages, onboarding, customer agreements, Document Room, billing workflow, AI workflows, and compliance review.

## Required Controls

- Maintain versioned terms with acceptance records.
- Clearly state product boundaries and limitations.
- Avoid unsupported promises about credit outcomes, banking services, lending, bureau access, or legal/accounting services.
- Require counsel review before publication.
- Track material changes and customer notices.

## Manual Tasks

- Approve terms with counsel.
- Configure acceptance capture and versioning.
- Define notice process for updates.
- Review website and marketing copy for consistency.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- Consent or e-signature capture.
- Customer notification service, if used.

## Evidence Needed

- Approved terms.
- Version history.
- Acceptance logs.
- Change notices.
- Audit logs.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires legal review before production reliance.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not validate any terms of service.
