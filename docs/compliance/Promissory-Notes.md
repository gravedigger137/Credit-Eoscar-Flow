# Promissory Notes

## Purpose

Define controls for drafting, storing, reviewing, and tracking promissory-note records without claiming that a generated note is legally effective.

## Scope

Covers principal amount, borrower, lender, interest, maturity, default terms, payment schedule, collateral reference, governing law, review status, versioning, and execution tracking.

## Applicable Module

Legal Instruments, Document Room, Credit Facility Readiness, Receivables and Collateral.

## Required Controls

- Require user-entered note terms and governing law.
- Keep note templates modular and versioned.
- Separate draft status from executed status.
- Link amortization schedules as references only, not proof of value.
- Require attorney review before marking a note approved or lender-visible.
- Audit all term changes and status changes.

## Manual Tasks

- Confirm parties and authority to sign.
- Confirm interest, usury, disclosure, and jurisdiction requirements with counsel.
- Attach execution evidence if executed.
- Review collateral references separately.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- E-signature or wet-signature process.
- Secure document storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Draft note.
- Signed note, if executed.
- Payment schedule.
- Governing-law selection.
- Approval record.
- Audit history.

## Status: Draft / Ready / Requires External Approval

Draft. Requires external legal approval before external reliance.

## Disclaimer

This documentation is not legal advice and does not create, validate, or enforce a promissory note.
