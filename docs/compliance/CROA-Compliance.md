# CROA Compliance

## Purpose

Document Credit Repair Organizations Act readiness controls for credit-repair service disclosures, customer agreements, cancellation rights, performance claims, and marketing review.

## Scope

Applies to Credit-Eoscar credit repair workflows, public booking, customer onboarding, service agreements, website copy, admin workflows, and customer communications.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar onboarding, customer agreements, Document Room, marketing review, support inbox, admin compliance queues, and audit logs.

## Required Controls

- Use counsel-reviewed customer agreements and disclosures.
- Track customer acceptance, cancellation window, authorization, and service activation status.
- Avoid unsupported credit-outcome promises.
- Maintain audit history for agreement changes, service status, and customer communications.
- Require review before marketing claims are published.

## Manual Tasks

- Review agreement templates with counsel.
- Approve marketing language.
- Define cancellation and refund handling procedures.
- Train admins on customer disclosures.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- E-signature provider, if used.
- Secure document storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Signed agreements.
- Disclosure acceptance records.
- Marketing review approvals.
- Cancellation/refund records.
- Audit logs.

Draft. Requires legal review before production credit-repair contracting.
