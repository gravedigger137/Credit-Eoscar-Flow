# Trust-Owned Business Records

## Purpose

Define controls for records showing that business interests, software assets, domains, contract rights, or other assets are claimed or tracked as trust-owned.

## Scope

Covers business registration evidence, assignments, asset schedules, corporate records, DBA/trade-name records, and supporting documents.

## Applicable Module

Master Asset Register, Trust Documents, Corporate Documents, Document Room.

## Required Controls

- Do not invent ownership or assignment status.
- Mark unsupported ownership as pending review.
- Attach source evidence for every asset record.
- Track attorney/accountant review fields.
- Audit assignment status, owner, assigned entity, and lender-visible changes.

## Manual Tasks

- Upload formation records, assignments, resolutions, and schedules.
- Confirm chain of title with counsel.
- Mask sensitive identifiers.
- Update asset register version history.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- Corporate records.
- State filing evidence.
- Tax/accounting review.

## Evidence Needed

- Assignment documents.
- Formation records.
- Corporate resolutions.
- Asset schedules.
- Review approvals.
- Audit trail.
