# Consumer Authorization Forms

## Purpose

Define controls for consumer authorization templates used for credit workflows, reports, disputes, payments, communications, and onboarding.

## Scope

Applies to customer onboarding, credit report authorization, dispute authorization, electronic communication consent, payment authorization, and service agreements.

## Applicable Module

Credit-Eoscar onboarding, Document Room, customer profile records, support inbox, compliance review, and audit logs.

## Required Controls

- Use versioned authorization templates.
- Track customer identity, acceptance timestamp, IP/device metadata where appropriate, authorization scope, revocation status, and document version.
- Keep authorization scope specific to the workflow.
- Require attorney review for consumer-facing authorization language.
- Do not rely on an authorization unless evidence is stored and linked to the customer record.

## Manual Tasks

- Review authorization templates with counsel.
- Configure e-signature or consent capture method.
- Define revocation handling.
- Train admins on scope limitations.

## External Dependencies

- Legal review.
- E-signature or consent capture service, if used.
- Secure storage.

## Evidence Needed

- Signed or accepted authorization.
- Template version.
- Consent metadata.
- Revocation records.
- Audit logs.

Draft. Requires legal review before production use.
