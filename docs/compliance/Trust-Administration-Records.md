# Trust Administration Records

## Purpose

Define controls for organizing trust administration records and trust-related workflow metadata.

## Scope

Covers trust records, governing jurisdiction, trustee/officer roles, resolutions, asset references, distribution records, review status, and audit history.

## Applicable Module

Document Room, Trust Documents, Asset Register, Officer Workflow Support, Compliance.

## Required Controls

- Record governing jurisdiction selected by the user.
- Keep trust terms, assets, administration tasks, and legal references separate.
- Require professional review for trust actions and interpretations.
- Track document versions and execution evidence.
- Audit changes to trustee roles, asset assignments, approvals, and lender-visible flags.

## Manual Tasks

- Upload governing trust documents.
- Confirm trustee authority and recordkeeping requirements with counsel.
- Maintain minutes, resolutions, and asset schedules.
- Review tax/accounting implications with professionals.

## External Dependencies

- Trust counsel.
- Tax/accounting professional.
- Secure document storage.
- Evidence retention.

## Evidence Needed

- Trust agreement or governing document.
- Resolutions.
- Asset schedules.
- Trustee/officer records.
- Review approvals.
- Audit logs.
