# Corporate Documents

## Overview
Corporate governance records for the corporation only, not the trust itself.

## Purpose
Organize bylaws, shareholder agreements, directors' organizational meeting records, resolutions, share registers, officer roles, banking resolutions, and minute book.

## Architecture
Use `legal_instruments` for bylaws, shareholder agreements, and resolutions. Use `document_room_items` for minutes and evidence.

## Dependencies
Corporate bylaws, shareholder agreement, board records, resolutions, officer list, share register, and banking records.

## Folder Structure
Document Room category: `Corporate`.

## Security
Mask sensitive ownership, tax, banking, and address data where appropriate.

## Maintenance
Update after board actions, officer changes, new shares, banking changes, or shareholder amendments.

## Related Documentation
`docs/due-diligence/02_Organizational_Documents.md`, `docs/Officer-Workflow-Support.md`.

## Required Documents
Bylaws, shareholder agreement, organizational meeting minutes, resolutions, share register, officer appointments, banking resolutions.

## Document Owner
Board/Resolution Drafting Agent with attorney review.

## Review Status
Draft - attorney review required.
